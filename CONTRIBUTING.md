# Contributing to Carcassonne: Lake Expansion

Thanks for your interest in helping improve this fan expansion. Here's how you can contribute.

## Playtesting

The most valuable contribution right now is playtesting feedback.

**What to play:**
- Mix the 25 lake tiles with the Carcassonne base game
- Play 2-4 player games (longer games surface more interesting decisions)
- Try at least one session with Inns & Cathedrals or Traders & Builders

**What to report:**
- How often lakes get completed vs. abandoned
- Whether fish distribution feels balanced (too few / too many)
- Any rules that were confusing or ambiguous
- Tiles that felt too easy or impossible to place
- Final scores — were lakes competitive with cities?

Open a GitHub issue with your findings or email feedback directly.

## Rules Feedback

If you spot an ambiguity or edge case not covered in [docs/rules.md](docs/rules.md), open an issue describing the situation and the ruling you'd expect.

## Tile Design

If you create tile artwork, share it as an issue or pull request. Please include:
- Source files (SVG, AI, or layered PNG)
- License information for any third-party assets used

## Bug Reports / Corrections

Typos, broken links, calculation errors — open an issue or submit a pull request.
